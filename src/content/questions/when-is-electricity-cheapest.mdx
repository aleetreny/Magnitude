Ask anyone in Spain when to put the washing on and they will say at night. The
price stopped agreeing three years ago.

In 2022 five months were cheapest at four in the morning. In 2025 only two are
— **January and December**, the darkest months of the year. Everywhere else the
hand has swung to the afternoon.

The day has two cheap windows now. The one before dawn is thinning. The one
after lunch shuts every winter and comes back wider. And between seven and ten
at night, not one cheap hour in four and a half years.

And they are pulling apart. The dearest hour cost **1.54 times** the cheapest in
2022, and **2.71 times** in 2025 — 81 € against 220 €.

The cheapest hour of the day used to be a habit. It has become a forecast.

<Method>

The price is the PVPC — the regulated tariff for small consumers, tolls and
charges included, which is what appears on the bill of anyone on it. Figures are
for the peninsular system; the Canaries and the Balearics are priced separately
and are not in this post.

The series begins on **1 June 2021**, the day the time-of-use PVPC came into
force. Ask the API for May of that year and it answers with an error, because
there is no such price to give. Nothing before that date is the same tariff, so
nothing before it is drawn. The year figures use complete calendar years only,
which is why 2021 has clocks but no line.

Hours are local clock hours, taken from the offset stamped on each reading —
the hour people live by rather than the one a server keeps. On the two clock
changes a year one hour goes missing and one happens twice; both are kept as
published.

A month's cheapest hour is the hour whose average price that month was lowest.
Night is 3, 4 and 5 in the morning; afternoon is 2, 3 and 4 in the afternoon.
Averaging by hour hides what a single day did: this is where the price tends to
sit, not a promise about tomorrow.

`npm run data:power` refetches the record a month at a time — the API refuses
longer ranges — into `data/source/`, and rebuilds `src/data/power-prices.json`
from it. The build refuses to write if any figure quoted in this post has moved.

</Method>
