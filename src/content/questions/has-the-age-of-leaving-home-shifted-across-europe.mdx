Every country in Europe has a door, and each one opens at a different age. What
almost none of them have is a door that moves.

In the survey that ran to 2020, the median country's whole range — its highest
reading minus its lowest — is **1.6 years**. Germany's is 0.4: every reading
from 2000 to 2020 landed between 23.7 and 24.1.

Across the same axis, Finland's door sits at 21.3 and Croatia's at 31.5. The
distance between two countries is more than six times everything a quarter
century did inside one.

Spain is at 30.2, and it has been at the late end for as long as the survey has
run: from 2000 to 2020 it never once left 28.3 to 29.8.

The age of leaving home reads like a fact about a generation. It behaves like a
fact about a country.

<Method>

Eurostat publishes this as an estimate, not a count: the age at which 50% of a
cohort no longer lives in a household with a parent, derived from the EU Labour
Force Survey. Figures are for both sexes and are the ones Eurostat published on
16 April 2026; nothing here is modelled on top of them.

Eurostat flags a **break in series** in 2021 — the labour force survey was
redefined — for 34 of the 36 territories in the dataset. A reading from 2020 and
one from 2021 are therefore not the same measurement, and no figure in this post
is a difference across that year. Every range quoted lies inside one survey, and
the chart draws the two surveys in separate lanes for the same reason.

A country appears if the older survey covered it at least ten times, the newer
one at least three, and it reported in the last year available. That drops the
United Kingdom, whose series ends in 2019, and Türkiye, whose series ends in
2024. Coverage of the older survey varies between 11 and 21 readings, so a
country's band is as wide as its own record, not as wide as the same span of
years everywhere.

Two of the bands are wide because a survey wobbled rather than because a country
did. Sweden's older readings fall steeply in the three years before the break, to
17.5; Portugal's first reading under the new survey is 33.3, and the next year's
is 30.1. Both are Eurostat's published figures and are drawn as published.

`npm run data:leaving-home` rebuilds `src/data/leaving-home.json` from the raw
API response kept in `data/source/`. It refuses to write if any figure quoted in
this post has moved.

</Method>
